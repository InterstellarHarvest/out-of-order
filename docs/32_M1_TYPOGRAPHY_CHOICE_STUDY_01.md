# M1 Typography + Choice Card Study 01
**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Status:** Pending owner visual selection; no typography lock yet.

Prepared a component-only study at the locked geometry. No full-frame regeneration and no layout changes.

## Shared choice-card prototype
- 548 px display width / 274 logical px.
- 80 / 108 / 136 / 164 px display heights.
- Rivet-free.
- Muted pale institutional inset with dark teal edge and restrained warm highlight.
- No dirt/stain variants.

## Typography candidates
A. **Condensed institutional** — Noto Sans Mono Condensed Medium used as a metric/style surrogate. Most compact and cleanest fit; example 2-line sample stayed at 2 lines.
B. **Typewriter bureaucratic** — Tlwg Typewriter surrogate. More period/bureaucratic personality but wider/airier; sample wraps more.
C. **Humanist terminal** — Go Mono surrogate. Friendly/readable but also wider and less institutional.

These installed fonts are **comparison surrogates**, not production font commitments. Once the owner selects a direction, PMO should identify/author an appropriately licensed pixel-font treatment with similar metrics and validate it against the fixed card/speech geometry.

## Recommended direction
PMO currently recommends **A / Condensed institutional** as the base typography direction because it preserves readability and dialogue depth without changing geometry. Add a small amount of pixel irregularity/period character in the final production face rather than widening the metrics.
