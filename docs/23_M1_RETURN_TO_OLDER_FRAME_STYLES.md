# M1 Frame Art — Return to Older Favored Designs (Owner correction)
**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Status:** Art-direction correction and new comparison studies; NOT a final frame-art lock.

## Why we returned
The immediately preceding more colorful, densely patched frame **FAILED** owner review: "too busy and silly" and "just looks like random junk." Adding many vents, colored plates, grille shapes and ornamental inserts independently across the frame is **not** meaningful *Brazil*-like absurdity.

The owner specifically directs the project to **return to earlier designs we liked** and APPLY those physical-art designs to the **current fixed M1 frame-only layout**. Do not redesign the geometry to fit older reference art.

## Older visual roots to explore
- **Civic Retrofit:** muted teal civic enamel and dark recessed UI, warm cream/brass fittings, localized purposeful repair.
- **Ceramic Committee:** pale institutional ceramic/cream panels with dark green molded Bakelite/steel and subtle asymmetry.
- **Amended Relay:** aged, darker functional painted steel/relay housing, carefully chosen worn joints and one evident later retrofit.
Prefer a coherent manufactured unit with a history, NOT a collage of different industrial gadget stickers.

## Shared unchanged layout contract
Current [canonical M1 contract](17_M1_CANONICAL_UI_DESIGN_CONTRACT.md) and [frame-only mode](21_M1_FRAME_ONLY_REVIEW.md) remain binding. 
- Full 16:9, left main scene ~70%, right rail ~30%; no top banner.
- ALL content vacant: empty scene, portrait, speech, choices, seven square inventory cells, and objective text body; only functional 'CURRENT OBJECTIVE' and 'INSPECT / NOTES / JOURNAL' labels.
- Portrait aperture AND speech aperture use same full right-rail width, slim symmetrical borders independent of scrollbar.
- Choice scrollbar with reserved narrow thumb track ONLY beside choice list below full-width speech, **no scrollbar arrows**.
- Seven compact square empty item slots with small separately housed arrows that shift ONE slot per press.
- Slim/footer sized by one square item slot plus modest rims: inventory LEFT, separate objective MIDDLE, three utility buttons RIGHT.
- No lettered decorative frame plates, no NPC/place-specific ornament, no pipes or decorative elements occluding content apertures.
- Palette limited but not monochrome; restrained muted olive/teal/cream, brass, old-rust and charcoal, not polychromatic junk.
- Distinctive slight irrationality is **structural** (repair plate placed oddly but purposefully, old retrofit seam, inappropriate but plausible mechanism), not scattered colorful parts.
- Crisp authored 16–32-bit-style pixel detailing; generated illustrations are exploratory, not verified pixel-perfect source sprites.

Three frame-only image studies generated from earlier layout/styles, delivered in conversation. These have similar overall metal treatment; they should be reviewed as a **directional reset** rather than evidence that style separation, final logical pixel grids, or a final frame lock are solved.
