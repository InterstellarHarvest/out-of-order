## Composition directive
**KEEP A as the master image/style for every part of the UI.** Borrow from **B only the NPC spoken-dialogue box treatment**—the functional cream inset rectangle *immediately below the full-width portrait*. B's speech box has a simpler, flatter institutional cream inset, smaller discreet corner fasteners, more restrained beveled border and muted even fill. By contrast A's box has a visibly more sculpted/chamfered and ornamental beveled perimeter. Blend B's modest, practical surface into A's teal/ivory brass material language.

Do **NOT** import from B:
- B's scroller hardware continuing next to the speech area (canonical scroller starts **strictly below speech**, beside choices ONLY; zero arrows).
- B's frame casing, choices, inventory, footer panels or dimensions.
- Extra pipes/vents/plates as a way to hide a mismatch.
- Any narrative content or NPC/scene illustrations.
