## 3. ART DIRECTION FOR THE FIVE DEATHS

### A — CLASSIFIED AS COMPOST

An automated Biology Park sorting bay after processing has finished.

Visual elements:
- Sealed organic-waste bale or collection bin.
- Quiet conveyor.
- Green completed-processing indicator.
- Small disposal receipt emerging from a printer.

The machinery regards the operation as entirely successful.

No person or recognizable remains.

### B — NEW ARRIVAL

A botanical nursery after the planting machinery has completed its task.

Visual elements:
- Planting hopper and mechanical arms at rest.
- Fresh, orderly mound of soil.
- Cheerful welcoming botanical marker.
- Artificial greenhouse lighting.

The disturbing implication is that the machinery classified a person as something to plant.

Avoid depicting an active planting operation or a visible body.

### C — PERSONAL GROWTH

A completed botanical transformation in a curated greenhouse.

Visual elements:
- Dense roots, leaves and flowers.
- An anonymous human-sized mass of vegetation.
- Cheerful successful-cultivation indicator.
- Controlled institutional greenhouse setting.

There must be no recognizable human face or person.

The transformation is complete, not ongoing.

This is the shared fatal outcome for its associated routes; do not invent separate illustrations for each cause.

### D — RECLASSIFIED

An industrial planting-machine processing station after completion.

Visual elements:
- Waste hatch.
- Planting hopper returned to standby.
- Neatly filled growing tray.
- Formal botanical processing marker or checkmark.
- Quiet automated handling arms.

**Distinguish this from NEW ARRIVAL.**

NEW ARRIVAL is a nursery welcome/planting aftermath.

RECLASSIFIED is an industrial sorting and reclassification aftermath.

The bureaucracy considers the item successfully reassigned.

No person or remains.

### E — SITE IMPROVEMENT

The underground service duct beneath the botanical translator, after roots have sealed it.

Visual elements:
- Dense roots completely occupying a narrow maintenance passage.
- Translator machinery overhead.
- A printer issuing another maintenance requisition.
- Possibly one small, anonymous shoe sole obscured behind roots, if visually appropriate.

No visible injury or identifiable body.

The humor comes from the machinery treating a fatal obstruction as routine infrastructure work.
