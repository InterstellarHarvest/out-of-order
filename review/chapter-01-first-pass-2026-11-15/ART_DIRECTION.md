## Shared production language

**Owner revision, 2026-11-15:** The House must look like a genuinely desirable 1960s exposition future home. Its machines create the disturbing behavior. It must **not match reception's colors or architectural design**. This ruling supersedes the assignment's earlier visual-continuity request. Three initial reception-like source renders are retained only under `generation-source/superseded-reception-direction/`; they are not candidates.

Warm ivory flush walls, honey/blond wood veneer, pale aqua molded plastic, restrained coral enamel details and thin brushed-aluminum channels establish the distinct domestic showroom. Warm light linoleum/terrazzo, low human-scale ceilings and slim integrated light panels replace reception's heavy green posts, industrial crossbeams, gray institutional floor and office ceiling grid. Rectangular rooms remain believable, with gentle curves on appliances and domestic furnishings. The building is bright, immaculate and inviting. Subtly replaced fittings show operation over decades; nothing looks haunted. Service areas may expose practical infrastructure behind this deliberately friendly finish.

Approved reception was inspected solely for locked production dimensions and pixel readability. Its design and palette are not supplied as generation references for the revised candidates. Reference file remains unchanged: `art/scenes/conference_hall/reception/reception_scene.png`, SHA-256 `fb010301d2df0c06d0cedaff6e35e60661e18764c9e5d9ed755ad78b0ac5fd53`.

House color anchors: warm ivory `#fff2d5`, offwhite `#f5f1e4`, pale aqua `#9fcfcb`, coral `#d78b6f`, blond wood `#c69b63`, soft slate `#53646a`, aluminum `#aeb6bc`; small charcoal grilles and encouraging green indicators. These are design anchors, not a fixed indexed-palette cap. Bathroom retains the specifically requested mint and chrome. Record measured colors per candidate.

Main scenes: exactly 314×206, opaque, chunky clusters and crisp stepped edges, reviewed native and at 4× nearest-neighbor (1256×824). Portraits: independent finer compositions at 564×278, displayed 1:1. No humans, skeletons, mannequins, family photographs or protagonist in main scenes. No heavy reception-style teal structure, institutional gray room palette, haunted-house lighting, grime, brass, steampunk, decorative gears, leather, cyberpunk, UI or watermarks. Operational signs favor nonletter marks; arrival title remains the named-sign exception.

## Continuity and camera plan

The narrative establishes connections, not a surveyed floor plan. The following is a proposed staging convention, not a geography edit: the arrival threshold and public exit are opposite views of the foyer's same ivory double door in a thin aluminum surround. Inside the foyer, scanner and articulated wall speaker share the entrance wall; a flush housekeeping hatch and drawer below hint at reachable storage. A pale aqua sliding partition leads into the domestic sequence. Dining and lounge retain this same thin trim, ivory panels and warm floor vocabulary. Bathroom fixtures sit against a closed mint service wall so initial artwork does not disclose missing pipes. Concealed service infrastructure lies behind the showroom panels. Bedroom wardrobe passage, chair breach and public exit remain separate routes; do not conflate their eventual Chapter 2 destinations.

The first batch shows baseline rooms, not every transient action. Doors closed in the foyer, housekeeping mechanism concealed; no surrendered item or adopted plant is baked in. Dining has four settings with a smaller child's setting, three family chairs visibly vacant and visitor's place nearest camera; all furniture is unoccupied. Bathroom is pristine and apparently usable. Narrative supplies moving partitions, passing badge, opening doors, swiveling speaker, concealed arm, personal possessions and fake-flush discovery.

## Production and review gate

Use built-in imagegen, preserve generated originals and exact prompts. Normalize deliberately pixel-like scene geometry only with exact-aspect crop and NEAREST to 314×206 per docs/54; never average scene pixels. Portrait background is separately generated. Transparent speaker uses real RGBA, cleaned crisp alpha and no hidden RGB outside sprite. Log every normalization step and measured dimensions/colors/hashes in the review manifest.

## Owner speaker revision

Wall speakers must read as 1960s domestic speakers, not air-conditioning vents. Use a rounded ivory case, dark circular woven-fabric grille over one speaker cone, narrow control area, green jewel lamp, modest aluminum rotary control, discreet camera aperture under case edge and short swivel foot. No horizontal louvers. Foyer, dining and transparent portrait share this design. Earlier vent-like images are preserved as superseded source renders, not final candidates.
