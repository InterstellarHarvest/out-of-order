# Decorative greenery and the named desk-plant escape

**Status: proposed art-direction clarification only. Not approved canon; not applied to art, story or gameplay.**

The approved House interiors contain decorative plants, while `plant_adoption` lets the carried desk plant become a named resident. The House says, “It's alive? And it has a name?” and then accepts it as somebody. Existing indoor greenery could make a player reasonably ask why it does not already count toward occupancy.

## Proposed clarification for owner review

Treat the House's fixed indoor ornamental greenery as **manufactured showroom dressing: artificial plants, not living household residents**. Preserve their appealing1960s display-home appearance. The carried white-pot desk plant is a real living object which the player names and hands over, triggering the House's mistaken welcome of a new family member. The misunderstanding and escape route remain intact.

This is a proposed interpretation, not a finding that existing images prove artificiality. It would need creative approval before being added to future art briefs, inspection descriptions or story notes. No hidden written notices, new occupancy rules, renamed decorative plants or retroactive story explanation are introduced here. Outdoor trees/landscaping seen through windows are outside the home's interior occupancy question.

## Scope of this revision

No existing plant is redrawn, removed or recolored. No story JSON, plant/item conditions, inventory handover, occupancy behavior or dialogue is changed. All currently reviewed room images remain available and unchanged. A future approved clarification could guide subsequent props or separately reviewed prose; it is not authorization to modify either now.
