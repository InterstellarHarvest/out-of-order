# Visual direction

Continue the owner-approved revised Batch 2 illustrated 16/32-bit pixel treatment. Exact selected references establish material, palette, scale and greenhouse engineering. Earlier chunky graphic style studies remain preserved in Batch 2 history and are not references for this batch.

The existing 64-color palette is reused without retraining or dithering. Industrial steel, slate, pale enamel, muted teal and botanical green dominate. Restrained red/orange marks identify paper stamps, instrument traces and hazards. The giant cream flower remains biological, with gold stamens rather than a face. No steampunk machinery, brass fantasy detailing or magical light sources.

A extends the orderly tower office with filing furniture, a reader/printer and bureaucratic records. B and C reuse the physiological rack identity established by the selected Sensor Gallery: three circular gauges, a red trace, lower cable sockets, heavy plant connections and paper equipment. B shows the full plant and district seal; C isolates the plant-to-paper chain with a normal loose tape run.

D/E/F share Groundskeeper pale-enamel housings, muted-green emblems, tracked/bulk landscaping construction and articulated mechanical parts. Their functions remain distinct: shallow cultivation bed with opposing arms, conveyor to compactor with high inspection hatch, and soil hopper tipped toward a pit beside irrigation. G uses the same bolted disposal-hatch and utility-pipe family from a low chute viewpoint.

Native silhouettes and exit/action affordances take priority over tiny labels. D/E unreadable sign lettering is replaced with deliberate administrative bars within recorded masks. Foliage and grating retain fine texture; B/C remain the busiest native-size scenes. No procedural art substitutes, blur, artificial dithering or broad post-generation restyling were used.

Later transit, conductor, exit, item, treatment and death-screen batches remain separate. Their layouts should inherit selected artwork only after owner selection. All seven images here remain generated, unselected.
