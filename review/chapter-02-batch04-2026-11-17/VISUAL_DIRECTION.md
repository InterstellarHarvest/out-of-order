# Visual direction

Continue the owner-approved revised Biology Park style from Batches 2/3: fine modeled 16/32-bit illustrated pixels, industrial steel/slate, pale enamel, muted teal and botanical green. Exact selected PNGs establish visual references. Rejected chunky graphic studies are not used.

The approved Tour Tram and Overgrown Tram establish transit materials, thick window frames, seats, handholds and route hardware. The reserve vehicle is deliberately distinct: a short squared maintenance car, pale upper body, worn sage/teal lower panels, rectangular windscreen, front service-cab controls and open side door. A/B/C carry that same new vehicle through siding, inactive interior and authored crash result.

The Courtesy Conductor is an original molded-plastic console concierge: cream oval face, dark round eyes, teal integral visor/brow, simple mouth, small molded nose and metal ear caps. B depicts that face recessed and inactive. C/E depict its authored activation and cheerful talking mouth. E adds only the telescoping neck/mount implied by its rise from the console. It has no new arms, legs, uniform, expressions or animation set. This intentional face exception does not give unrelated machines faces.

The Groundskeeper remains a landscaping machine with hopper and planting equipment, not a friendly driver bust. Its selected portrait is copied only as an identity-distinction reference and remains unchanged.

C/D show the same concourse collision: torn cream/teal/red advertising graphics, pale steel piers, public spectators and modest debris. A/B do not contain crash or evacuation results. F is a quiet underground service station with a closed gate, separated from the bright greenhouse transit spaces.

All native candidate colors belong to the exact existing64-color palette; E uses its visible subset. No palette retraining, blur or dithering. Tiny notice text is simplified locally rather than forced into illegible words. Fine foliage, wall damage and audience silhouettes remain dense at 314×206; creative selection remains separate from file validation.
